Selected work

theia Rust · P2P overlay + services

A public-key-addressed, control-plane-free peer-to-peer overlay I architected and built solo. Reach any machine by its ed25519 identity, across NATs, with no address, account, or server in between. I generalized the transport behind a single trait: it runs on iroh or on a QUIC-style UDP transport I wrote from scratch, proven substitutable under one conformance suite that ships decoy transports, so it can't pass by accident. On top of that: capability auth where the key is the identity (offline-verifiable, replay-safe at compile time), and keyless SSH, file transfer, and link measurement behind one CLI.

A family of Rust crates: bifrost (the transport trait + conformance suite), nauthy (capability auth on biscuit and ed25519), tightbeam (capability-gated tunnels), and swoosh (the CLI that ties it together).
